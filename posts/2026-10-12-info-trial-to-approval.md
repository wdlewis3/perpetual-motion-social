## Caption
How does a psychedelic medicine become an approved treatment? Here is the basic path.

Small early studies look at safety. Larger trials compare the treatment against a control group to see whether it works. Then the sponsor submits an application, and the FDA weighs the benefits against the risks and can ask for more trials. Approval is also not the only step: federal drug scheduling and state rules can follow separately, and they vary.

For veterans and patients, trials are how a regulated, lawful option gets built. Stigma slows that work and keeps people from talking to a licensed clinician about their options.

Educational only. Not medical or legal advice. Laws vary by state; talk to a licensed clinician. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line).
