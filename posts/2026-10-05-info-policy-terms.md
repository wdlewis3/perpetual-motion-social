## Caption (paste-ready)

Psychedelic policy headlines can sound like one thing when they are really four different things.

Funding: a bill that pays for research pays for studies. It does not change what is legal.
Designation: an FDA designation such as breakthrough therapy can speed development and review. It is not an approval.
Approval: this is what allows a drug to be prescribed as a medicine, under its own rules for how it is given.
State program: a state can set up its own framework, and how that fits with federal law is a separate question.

Knowing which one you are reading helps veterans and families judge what a headline changes today and what it doesn't. Stigma makes it harder, because people hear "psychedelic" and assume the worst, or assume it is already available. Clear terms make lawful care easier to find.

Educational only. Not medical or legal advice. Laws vary by state; talk to a licensed clinician. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line).

Which of these terms have you seen mixed up in the news?
