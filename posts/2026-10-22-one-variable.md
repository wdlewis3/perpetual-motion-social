## Caption (paste-ready)

I was trained to change one thing at a time.

Years in a chemistry lab teach you that if you change five variables at once, you can't say which one made the difference. Coaching works the same way. When someone overhauls their sleep, food, training, and schedule in a single week, they usually can't tell what helped, and the whole thing is hard to keep up.

So we start with one change that fits your real week. We run it long enough to see how it goes, adjust, then add the next one. It's slower at the start and it holds up better.

That's most of how I coach: one clear change, honest check-ins, and a plan built around your life.
