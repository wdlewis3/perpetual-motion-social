## Caption (paste-ready)

One missed day doesn't break a habit. Two in a row starts a new one.

Most people treat a skipped workout or a missed meal prep as proof the plan failed. It isn't. Everyone misses days. What decides the outcome is what you do the next morning.

Here is a simple rule to try. If you miss a day, the next day is non-negotiable, even if it's a smaller version. A ten-minute walk counts. One set counts. You are protecting the pattern, not chasing a perfect streak.

It also helps to decide the restart in advance. Write down what "back on track" looks like before you need it, so you aren't negotiating with yourself on a tired Tuesday.
