## Caption
A lot of headlines use "designation," "voucher," "filing," and "approval" as if they mean the same thing. They don't.

An FDA designation like Breakthrough Therapy is meant to help a promising treatment move through development and review more efficiently. It does not mean the treatment is approved, proven, or available. The evidence standard for safety and effectiveness stays the same.

For veterans and patients, the difference matters. Approval is one step. Federal scheduling, trained clinicians, coverage, and state or VA rules all affect real access. Part of what keeps people from good information is that these terms get blurred, so reading the exact wording of an announcement is worth the minute.
