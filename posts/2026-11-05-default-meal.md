## Caption (paste-ready)

Pick a default for the meal that goes wrong most often.

For most people it's one specific meal. Maybe it's the breakfast you skip, the lunch you grab between meetings, or the late dinner after a long day. Whichever one it is, you usually end up deciding about it when you're already hungry, and that's the worst time to decide.

So decide ahead of time. Choose one simple default for that meal, something you can make or buy without thinking, with a reasonable amount of protein and something you actually like. Keep the ingredients on hand and use it on the days you have nothing else planned.

You can still eat differently whenever you want to. The default just means a busy day doesn't leave you starting from zero.
