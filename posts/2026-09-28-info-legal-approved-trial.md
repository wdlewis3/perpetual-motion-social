## Caption (paste-ready)
Three phrases get mixed up in psychedelic headlines: decriminalized, legal, and FDA approved. They are not the same thing.

Decriminalized generally means a local or state government has deprioritized enforcement. When a state calls something legal, it can mean a specific state program with its own rules. FDA approved means a medicine went through clinical trials and the agency reviewed the safety and effectiveness data for a specific use. A clinical trial is different again: a closely monitored study with screening and clinical oversight.

For veterans and patients, a headline about one of these doesn't tell you the others have changed. Rules differ between federal and state levels, and status can change, so check current sources.

Mixing up these terms is part of how stigma sticks. People either dismiss the research or assume access is easier than it is.

Which of these terms have you seen mixed up most?
