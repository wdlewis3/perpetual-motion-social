## Caption (paste-ready)

Most lifters don't need to train harder. They need to stop one rep short.

If every set ends in a grind, form gets sloppy, recovery drags, and the next session starts from behind. Leaving one or two reps in reserve keeps the movement clean and lets you show up again in two days.

Here is a simple way to try it. Pick a weight you could lift for a few more reps than the number on your program. Finish each set knowing you had a little left. When it starts to feel easy, add a small amount of weight the next week, not a big jump.

Hard sessions still have a place. They just work better when most of your training is repeatable.
