## Caption
Before anyone enters psychedelic research or care, there is a step that rarely gets attention: screening.

Responsible programs look at your health history, current medications, and heart health before anything else. Some prescriptions interact with these compounds, and some mental health histories call for extra caution. A licensed clinician is the person who sorts that out.

Preparation and integration coaching is a different role. It helps with planning and follow-through. It does not diagnose, treat, or supply anything.

When you look at any program or facilitator, ask who is licensed, how screening is done, and what the plan is if something goes wrong.

Educational only. Not medical or legal advice. Laws vary by state; talk to a licensed clinician. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line).
