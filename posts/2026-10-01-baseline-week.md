## Caption (paste-ready)

Most people start a new routine by changing everything at once. A better first step is to spend one week just writing down what you already do.

Note when you wake up, when you eat, when you move, and when you go to bed. Don't judge it and don't fix it yet. You are only collecting information.

At the end of the week, look for the one place where a small change would fit easily. Maybe it's a meal that keeps getting skipped, or a bedtime that drifts later each night. Start there.

A plan built on what your week actually looks like holds up much better than one built on what you wish it looked like.
