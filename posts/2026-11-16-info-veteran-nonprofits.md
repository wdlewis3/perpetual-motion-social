## Caption (paste-ready)

Several nonprofits exist specifically to help veterans reach psychedelic care. Their names come up often, so it helps to be clear about what that work usually looks like.

Most of them are not clinics and do not provide any substance. The common model is connecting veterans with programs that operate lawfully where they are located: a clinical trial, a state-regulated program, or a program in a jurisdiction where it is legal. Around that, they typically offer screening, preparation, integration support, and peer community. Some also fund scholarships, support research, or train facilitators.

That matters because a veteran who hears "psychedelics" and pictures something underground may never look further. Knowing that screened, structured, lawful routes exist is often what makes it possible to ask a clinician a real question.

Before trusting any organization, check its nonprofit status, where its programs run and under what law, how it screens, and what support follows.

Educational only. Not medical or legal advice. Laws vary by state; talk to a licensed clinician. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line).
