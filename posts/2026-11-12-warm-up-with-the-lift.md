## Caption (paste-ready)

Warm up with the bar, not the treadmill.

Easy cardio raises your heart rate, but it doesn't teach your body the movement you're about to load. If the session is squats, warm up with squats. Start with the empty bar, then add weight in a few steps until you reach your first working set.

Each of those lighter sets does two jobs. It rehearses the position, so your first heavy rep isn't also your first rep. And it tells you how today feels. Some days the bar moves well and you follow the plan. Some days a weight that is usually easy feels slow, and you adjust before you load up.

That feedback is the point. A warm-up that doesn't give you any isn't doing much.
