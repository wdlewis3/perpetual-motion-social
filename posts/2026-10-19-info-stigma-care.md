## Caption (paste-ready)

Stigma around psychedelic research gets talked about as if it only affects how people feel. It also affects how fast good information reaches the people who need it.

When a topic carries shame, fewer people ask their clinician about it, fewer studies get funded and published without a fight, and more people end up piecing things together from rumor instead of evidence. For veterans and first responders, who already face a lot of hesitation about asking for mental health support, that gap matters.

The way this field gets answered is not complicated: clinical trials, regulatory review by agencies like the FDA, and care from licensed clinicians. Reading what those sources actually say, and not what a headline implies, is a good starting point.

What's one thing you wish more people understood about how this research works?
